# Customer Proof Sprint

Turn a narrowly defined pilot result into a customer story that a buyer can evaluate.

The central challenge is not writing an impressive percentage. It is showing the source, denominator, measurement scope, and limits behind that percentage.

## Inspect the work

1. [Read the fictional customer story, proof slide, and seller examples](sample.md).
2. [Use the evidence-first drafting prompt](prompt.md).
3. [Review the claim-quality evaluation cases](evaluation.md).
4. Run `python3 verify.py` to reproduce the time calculations.

The sample compares 120 quote drafts requiring 5,400 preparation minutes with another 120 requiring 2,160 minutes. That supports averages of 45 and 18 minutes, a 60% reduction in the recorded average, and a 54-hour difference between batches. It does not establish payroll savings or sole causality.

## Design choices

- Keep the time result in time units because there is no evidence of realized financial benefit.
- Preserve estimator review and pricing approval in the workflow description.
- Put the fictional status in the headline area and source ledger.
- Produce different buyer-facing messages from the same bounded evidence.
- Keep missing measurements visible instead of replacing them with plausible numbers.

## Origin and status

Adapted from an existing September 2026 AI-assisted service-concept sample. The narrative is retained; the reusable prompt, arithmetic check, and evaluation cases were added during portfolio packaging. Evaluation cases are expected behaviors for manual/model review, not a claim of completed live-model testing. No customer work or paid engagement is claimed.
