# Pilot charter template

| Decision field | Complete before launch |
|---|---|
| Business question | Which task or service constraint are we trying to change? |
| Scope | Sites, task boundaries, operators, schedule, exclusions |
| Baseline | Period, volume, staff minutes, downtime, corrections, comparability limits |
| Ownership | Executive sponsor, operating owner, Finance reviewer, deployment lead |
| Readiness | Site suitability, training, support, access requirements, incident escalation |
| Commercial terms | Paid scope, delivery obligations, agreed treatment of any pilot credit |
| Success measures | Customer-agreed thresholds, definitions, data source, measurement frequency |
| Financial evidence | Actual avoided expense or incremental contribution, all implementation and recurring costs |
| Capacity evidence | Time released, where it is redeployed, utilization, service quality |
| Expansion | Decision date, decision-maker, pricing assumptions, service capacity, next-site conditions |
| Stop conditions | Unresolved safety, data quality, adoption, service, or economic issues |

## End-of-pilot decision memo

- **Decision:** expand / revise / stop / insufficient evidence.
- **Supported result:** specify measurement period, denominator, and evidence reference.
- **Limits:** identify confounders and missing data.
- **Customer acceptance:** record who accepted the measurement and when.
- **Economics:** separate projected benefits from realized benefits; do not count the same time as both labor savings and new revenue.
- **Delivery capacity:** name the owner and available capacity for the next phase.
- **Next commitment:** owner, due date, resource requirement, and next decision.
