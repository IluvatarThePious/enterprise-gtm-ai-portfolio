# Manager review cadence

This is a proposed operating routine, not evidence of prior direct-report management.

## Weekly: one decision per opportunity

The seller brings the buyer's problem, current evidence, biggest unknown, named owner, and dated next step. The manager asks which new evidence supports stage progression. Activity counts provide context; they do not substitute for qualification.

Record:

| Opportunity | Stage evidence | Critical unknown | Next evidence | Owner | Due date | Capacity issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fictional site group B | Sponsor identified | Support coverage | Deployment review | Operations lead | Before launch | Second site unresolved |

## Every two weeks: coach one observable behavior

Pick a discovery or business-case example, identify one decision the seller handled well and one behavior to change, then review an actual subsequent example. Record what changed rather than assuming the coaching caused a revenue result.

## Monthly: compare commercial and operational evidence

- Pilot-to-expansion conversion: expanded eligible pilots divided by decision-complete eligible pilots; keep pending pilots separate.
- Forecast error: compare the fixed-date forecast snapshot with actual bookings using consistent period and currency.
- Service burden: deployment/support hours per launched site, segmented by rollout stage.
- Adoption: actual eligible use versus agreed scheduled use, with exclusions stated.
- Margin: use Finance's agreed definition and include relevant delivery costs.

Use adverse results to revise account criteria, offer scope, training, or resource allocation. Record the decision and the evidence needed to revisit it.
