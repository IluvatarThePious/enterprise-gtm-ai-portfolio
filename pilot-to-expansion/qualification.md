# Account qualification rubric

Illustrative prioritization weights, adjustable to the business. Scores are judgments, not measured probabilities.

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence required for a high score |
|---|---:|---|
| Task and problem fit | 25 | Defined repeatable task; important customer problem |
| Measurement access | 20 | Baseline records available; comparable pilot measurement possible |
| Buying alignment | 20 | Operating owner, sponsor, Finance participant, credible budget path |
| Deployment readiness | 25 | Site, operator training, service coverage, support ownership confirmed |
| Expansion repeatability | 10 | Similar next sites; rollout decision and constraints understood |

Rate each dimension 0–5 and calculate `sum(weight × rating / 5)`. Unknown evidence receives 0 and an explicit follow-up, rather than an invented midpoint. A 0 is an evidence gap, not proof that an account is unattractive.

**Hard gates override the total:** do not launch when required site/safety reviews, authorized data access, operational ownership, or essential service capacity are missing.

Fictional comparison:

| Candidate | Fit | Measurement | Buying | Readiness | Expansion | Total | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Site group A | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 3 | 83 | Prioritize after verifying hard gates |
| Site group B | 5 | 3 | 4 | 1 | 5 | 66 | Resolve service-readiness gap before launch |

Sensitivity: with equal 20-point weights, both score 80 and 72 respectively. A still ranks first; the second account's hard gate remains regardless of weights.
